Amazon Purchase Bot - Terms of Use

WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES

This application automates your own Amazon account using an ordinary browser
window that it controls on your computer. It can read a product page, check
the price, availability, seller and condition, and - when you tell it to -
place an order through Amazon's normal checkout.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

You remain responsible for your Amazon account and for complying with
Amazon's Conditions of Use and any other terms that apply to it. You are
responsible for every order placed through this software, including orders
placed by the automatic purchasing option if you switch it on.

Check your rules before enabling automatic purchasing, and keep Test Mode on
until you have confirmed the software behaves the way you expect.

WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES NOT DO

It does not store your Amazon password, your one-time codes, your passkeys or
your card details. You sign in directly with Amazon in its own browser
window.

It does not attempt to bypass CAPTCHAs, defeat bot detection, disguise itself
as something other than browser automation, rotate network addresses or
falsify device information. When Amazon asks for a human, the software stops
and asks you.

It sends no data anywhere. There is no telemetry, no analytics and no crash
reporting. Everything it records stays in a folder on this computer.
